Note: This application is intended for use in France only.

Sentinelle is an Android app that protects you from unwanted calls and SMS. It is a fork of the open-source Saracroche project (GPLv3), evolving into a broader on-device protection app: spam call/SMS blocking, a local statistics &amp; history dashboard, and an on-device heuristic (rule-based) spam-scoring engine.

<b>Features:</b>
• Automatically blocks spam numbers and SMS
• Local statistics &amp; history dashboard for blocked calls/SMS
• On-device heuristic spam scoring, with no data ever leaving the device
• Native Android application with modern Material Design 3 UI
• Privacy-respectful: nothing is uploaded off-device; any local history used for scoring is opt-in and stays on-device
• Regular updates of the spam number database

<b>Privacy &amp; Security:</b>
Sentinelle prioritizes your privacy. The app operates locally on your device and doesn't collect or upload any contact list, call log, or personal data. All spam detection — including the optional heuristic scoring — is performed entirely on-device.

<b>How it works:</b>
The app uses Android's Call Screening Service to automatically identify and block known spam numbers before they can disturb you. When a call comes in, Sentinelle checks the number against its local spam database and, optionally, an on-device heuristic score, then blocks it if identified as spam.

<b>Requirements:</b>
• Android 10 (API level 29) or higher
• Permission to be set as the default Phone app for call screening
• Intended for use in France only

<b>Open Source:</b>
Sentinelle is open source software and a derivative work of Saracroche (GPLv3) by cbouvat. You can review the code, contribute, or build it yourself from the source.
